Who we are

A gathering of believers who meet regularly, and on Sundays, to seek and fellowship with God.

Seeking God with a consistent pattern

Seeking God grows strong when it follows a consistent pattern. So alongside our Sunday gathering, we run weekly programs that help students discover God and discover themselves in Him.

Most of these programs are run by the leaders of our various departments, who are committed and willing to serve.

Weekly programs

Six regular gatherings that build a consistent pattern in seeking God.

Every week, a pattern for seeking God

Seeking God becomes a habit when it follows a consistent pattern. These programs help students discover God and discover themselves in Him. They are run by the leaders of each department, who are committed and willing to serve.

Bible study

Open the Word together and learn how to live it out.

Discipleship

Grow into a mature and devoted follower of Christ.

Prayer meetings

Bring your burdens, your studies and your future to God.

Praise and worship practice

Rehearse, sing and prepare to lead the CU into God's presence.

Mentorship hour

Learn from leaders and teachers who walk ahead of you in faith.

Fellowship meetings

Meet, encourage one another and grow together as brothers.

Days and times for each program are announced by the department leaders. Ask any CU leader if you are not sure where to start.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Christian Union.

What is the Christian Union?

A gathering of believers who meet regularly, and on Sundays, to seek and fellowship with God. We aim to nurture mature and devoted disciples of Christ.

When do we meet?

We meet every Sunday. Our departments also run weekly programs, and their leaders announce the days and times.

What weekly programs are there?

Bible study, discipleship, prayer meetings, praise and worship practice, mentorship hour and fellowship meetings.

What is a Weekend of Change?

A special program where students gather throughout the weekend to receive from God and be transformed into great men.

What is KSCF?

The Kenya Students Christian Fellowship is one of the main organizations working with our Christian Union. It supports our programs and creates opportunities such as rallies and long holiday camps.

Who leads the Christian Union?

Our patron, Dr. Jane, and co-patron, Mr. Danson, lead alongside our teachers and the leaders of each department. The school administration, led by Principal Mr. Morris Thigaa, supports our work.

How can I serve?

Ask a department leader how to join a department such as praise and worship, prayer or Bible study.
